Privacy policy

Effective November 1, 2026. Ultra is run by Joshua Cai. Questions: joshuacai2701@gmail.com.

Ultra records your runs, rates you as a distance runner and builds you a training plan. This page says what we collect, why, who sees it and how to delete it. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads and the app contains no third-party tracking or analytics.

What we collect

  • Account: your username, display name and a password (stored only as a salted hash). If you use Sign in with Apple, we receive an Apple account identifier and, if you choose to share it, your name and email or Apple's private relay address. Profile and banner photos if you add them.
  • Runs: the GPS track of each run you record, with time, distance, pace, elevation and, if available, heart rate and cadence.
  • Apple Health (only if you allow it): during runs we read heart rate, and we read your body weight to estimate calories. If you choose Import runs, we read your past running workouts from Health, with their GPS routes and heart rate, and save them to your Ultra account. We save runs you record in Ultra to Health as workouts. Health data is used only to show your own runs, records, rating and plan. It is never used for advertising, never sold and never shared with anyone else.
  • Training plan: the goal race, date, target time and weekly running you enter when you make a plan.
  • Device and diagnostics: a push notification token if you allow notifications, and crash and error reports (app version, iOS version, what failed). Error reports never include your location, password or email.

How we use it

  • To record and show your runs, history, records and splits.
  • To compute your World Running Number (WRN) and the paces in your training plan.
  • To suggest running routes. Route suggestions use your current location only to answer that request.
  • To send notifications you asked for, keep the service secure, and fix crashes. Morning run reminders are scheduled on your phone and don't send anything to us.

Who can see what

This version of Ultra has no feed, friends or public leaderboards. Your runs, history and plan are shown only to you in the app. Your username, display name, photos and WRN are part of your profile, which other signed-in Ultra users may see if features for racing or following other runners are turned on. If we add those features, we'll update this policy and tell you in the app first.

Who we share it with

Only the providers that run the service for us, under contract and only for that purpose: our server and database host (Oracle Cloud) and Apple (for push notifications and Sign in with Apple). Map tiles come from Apple Maps. Route data comes from OpenStreetMap and NYC Open Data; we don't send them anything about you. We disclose data to authorities only when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and runs until you delete them. Error reports are kept for up to 90 days. Database backups are kept for up to 7 days and then overwritten.

Your choices

  • Delete any run from its menu.
  • Turn morning run reminders off in Settings.
  • Change location, Motion, Health and notification permissions in iOS Settings at any time.
  • Delete your account in Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, every run (including imported ones), your WRN and your training plan from our servers. Runs in Apple Health stay in Health, which you control.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data or to correct it at joshuacai2701@gmail.com.

Children

Ultra isn't for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Passwords and sign-in tokens are stored only as hashes. No system is perfectly secure, and we'll tell you if a breach affects your data.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
